# Creston PPS x CymaSpace Workshops 2025-26 Project Scope

Prep guide and resources for Creston x CymaSpace "SeeSound" Workshops on 9/17, 10/22, 12/10, 2/11, 3/11, 4/22, 5/13.

## 🎯 Objectives

2025-2026 School Season Monthly 2 hour Workshops for 4 groups of K-K5 students in rotation with hands on 25 minute STEAM activity (likely #education-seesound)

### Workshop Structure
- Workshops take place on "Early Release Days" at Creston Public School (during the morning)
- 4x K-K5 Age-Range Student Groups per Workshop
- Estimated 5 Students Per Group
- Each group spends 25 minutes engaging with hands-on interactivity
- Must have a "Music" component to the workshop due to PPS funding source, along with the Multisensory "STEAM" component
- Communication Access: Accommodate all styles of communication and hearing abilities
- Crestone School to Provide 3x hours of ASL interpreters per workshop

## Proposed Activity

"SeeSound" LED Tubes such as CymaTubes or similar devices in our inventory pulling from projects pertaining to #tech-cymatic-leds & #tech-audiolux-library & #committee-universalmusic -- Not a haptic event*

*Justification: SeeSound set-ups are portable, self-contained, do not require complex tech set-up beyond charging devices and needing sound/music sources playing over speakers in the room. The budget does not afford the complexity and logistics of haptic vest rental.
